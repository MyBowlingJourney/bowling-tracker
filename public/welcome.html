MY BOWLING JOURNEY

Every shot, not just every score.

Log what actually happened on each shot while you are still standing on the approach — which ball, where it left, what you did about it. Then find out what your arsenal, your spares and your season really look like.

Open the app
Works in your browser. No install needed to start.

What it does

  • Shot-by-shot logging built for league night — fast enough to keep up between frames.
  • Your arsenal, ball by ball: what each one actually carries and leaves.
  • Spares and splits, including which leaves you keep missing and from where.
  • Leagues, teams and tournaments, with the formats bowlers really use.
  • Houses and patterns — how you score where you score it.
  • A read-back of your night when it ends, in plain English.

Free, and Pro

Your own numbers are free and stay free: your scores, your averages, your spare and strike percentages, your ball-by-ball figures. Pro is for the comparisons — you against a teammate, one ball against another, this season against last — and for the parts that read your night for you.

Free includes

One league, unlimited practice and open bowling, every shot you log kept for as long as you want it, and all of your own statistics.

Pro adds

Every league and team you bowl in, head-to-head and teammate comparisons, ball-against-ball and house-against-house, season-against-season, scorecard import, and the AI features — Nightcap, Insights, Brooklyn, the Caddie and coaching.

Before you subscribe

  • Every new account has Pro free for 60 days from the day it is created, with no card asked for. When it ends you choose: subscribe, or carry on with the free Basic plan.
  • A subscription starts on the day you buy it and renews automatically — monthly or yearly — until you cancel.
  • Cancel any time. In the Android app, that is the Play Store app under Subscriptions. On the web, it is the link in your receipt or an email to us. Cancelling stops the next renewal; you keep Pro until the end of the period you have already paid for.
  • Refunds. We do not refund part-used periods as a rule, because you keep access until the period ends. If you were charged by mistake, or something was broken and we could not fix it, write to us and we will make it right. Purchases made through Google Play are also subject to Google Play's own refund policy.
  • Prices are in US dollars. In Canada they are in Canadian dollars — $6.99 a month or $69.99 a year — and in Japan, Singapore, Malaysia, the Philippines, Mexico, South Korea, Hong Kong, India, the United Arab Emirates and Costa Rica they are in your own currency with tax included, in the app and on the web alike. In the Android app elsewhere, Google Play shows the price in your own currency. If tax applies where you are, it is shown before you confirm.
  • If you stop paying, nothing you logged is deleted. One league stays active and the rest are paused until you come back.
