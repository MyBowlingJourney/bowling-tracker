How it works

Your league night, in more detail than the scoreboard keeps

Every screen below is the real app.

Log it before you leave the approach

Tap the result, then add as much as you want: the ball, the kind of strike — flush, light, messenger, Brooklyn — your stand and target boards, speed and more. Everything past the result is optional, so it keeps up between frames.

The scoresheet keeps itself

Every frame shows the rack you actually left, with your series so far and the most you can still bowl. Scoring for teammates too? Keep everyone on one phone.

Find the pins that are costing you

Every leave you keep seeing, how often it shows up and how often you convert it — single pins, the 10, splits and the rest. With Pro, see it side by side with your team.

Watch your line move through the blockPro

Each ball's path drawn on a real lane: 39 boards, arrows at 15 feet, the breakpoint set by the oil pattern. Slide through the night frame by frame and see where you moved.

Nightcap reads your night back to youPro

Save the night and Nightcap tells you what mattered in plain English. Your theory series shows the pins you left on the lane.

Every milestone, kept

Your first 800, fifty nights, the big four you finally picked up. Your journey remembers them, and shows you what's next.

Before you subscribe

  • Every new account has Pro free for 60 days from the day it is created, with no card asked for. When it ends you choose: subscribe, or carry on with the free Basic plan.
  • A subscription starts on the day you buy it and renews automatically — monthly or yearly — until you cancel.
  • Cancel any time. In the Android app, that is the Play Store app under Subscriptions. On the web, it is the link in your receipt or an email to us. Cancelling stops the next renewal; you keep Pro until the end of the period you have already paid for.
  • Refunds. We do not refund part-used periods as a rule, because you keep access until the period ends. If you were charged by mistake, or something was broken and we could not fix it, write to us and we will make it right. Purchases made through Google Play are also subject to Google Play's own refund policy.
  • Prices are in US dollars. In Canada they are in Canadian dollars — $6.99 a month or $69.99 a year — and in Japan, Singapore, Malaysia, the Philippines, Mexico, South Korea, Hong Kong, India, the United Arab Emirates, Costa Rica and Taiwan they are in your own currency with tax included, in the app and on the web alike. In the Android app elsewhere, Google Play shows the price in your own currency. If tax applies where you are, it is shown before you confirm.
  • If you stop paying, nothing you logged is deleted. One league stays active and the rest are paused until you come back.
