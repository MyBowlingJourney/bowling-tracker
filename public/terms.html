Some features share your data with other bowlers, but only ones you accept, and they differ in scope:

8.AI features, and their limits

Scorecard import, Insights and the in-app assistant use AI, and AI gets things wrong.

  • Scorecard import reads scores from a photo. It can misread them. Check imported scores before relying on them, especially for anything that decides a match, a handicap or a payout.
  • Insights produces observations about your own statistics. They are descriptions of numbers, not coaching, and not predictions.
  • The assistant answers questions about bowling. It can be confidently wrong.
