Privacy Policy

Effective 18 September 2026 · Last updated 18 September 2026

My Bowling Journey is run by My Bowling Journey LLC, a Pennsylvania limited liability company. This policy explains exactly what the app collects, why, who else can see it, and how to get rid of it.

The short version

We collect your email address so you can sign in, and the bowling data you enter. We ask for your location only when you search for a bowling center, and for a photo only when you import a scorecard. We do not sell your data, we do not show advertising, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

You can export everything from Settings at any time, and you can have it all deleted — see Deleting your account.

WhatWhenWhy

What the error diagnostics actually contain

When the app hits an error, it records what kind of error it was, where in the app it happened, an error code, and a count of how many times it has occurred. Error messages are redacted before they are stored, and reports are only sent for signed-in accounts. We do not capture your screen, your keystrokes, or the contents of what you were entering.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for advertising or for anyone else's marketing.

What other bowlers can see

Nothing you record is visible to another bowler unless you set up a connection that shares it. There are exactly three, and they differ in how much they share:

ConnectionWhat they can see
Teammates Your bowler name, and the sessions and shots you recorded for that team — those only. Your practice, your other leagues and any other teams stay private.
Friends Once you both accept, each of you can see the other's sessions and shots. This is not limited to a shared team or league — it is your recorded bowling generally.
A coach you accept Your sessions and shots, and any notes they write for you. Also not limited to one league.

Being in the same league is not sharing

A league is a label on your own records. Someone in your league who is not your teammate, your friend, or your accepted coach cannot see your name or your scores through the app.

None of these connections happen without you accepting them, and you can end them. Because friends and coaches see more than teammates do, it is worth only accepting those from people you are happy to show your bowling to.

How long we keep it

Your data stays for as long as your account exists, because a bowling history is only useful over time. When you delete your account we delete your data, other than anything we are required to keep for security, fraud prevention or legal compliance — which in practice means limited records of abuse or misuse, kept no longer than necessary.

One thing deletion cannot reach: if another bowler recorded something about you in their own records — a teammate logging a match you both bowled, for instance — that belongs to their account, not yours, much as a printed scoresheet would. Your own records, your name and your account are all deleted.

Deleting your account and your data

How to delete everything

Email support@mybowlingjourney.com from the address on your account and ask us to delete it. We will confirm, then delete your account and associated data within 30 days.

Full instructions are on the account deletion page.

Export first if you want a copy. Settings → Export gives you everything you have recorded, as a file you keep. Deletion cannot be undone, and we cannot recover your history afterwards.

Your choices

  • Location — your device asks before the app can use it, and you can refuse or revoke it in your device settings. Center search still works using your saved home center.
  • Photos — you choose each image individually. The app never browses your library.
  • Export — available any time from Settings.
  • Deletion — see above.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit using HTTPS. Access to your data is restricted to your own account by database-level security rules. No system is perfect, and we will not pretend otherwise — but we do not store passwords at all, because signing in works by emailed link rather than a password you could reuse elsewhere.

Children

Children under 13 should not create their own account. The app is not directed at them and we do not knowingly collect personal information from a child who has signed up on their own. If a child has created an account without a parent's or guardian's knowledge, email us and we will delete it — you do not need to explain yourself.

Tracking your child's bowling is a different thing, and it's fine

Plenty of parents want to keep a record of a young bowler's games. You can do that under your own account, exactly as you would on a paper scoresheet — add them as a bowler and record their games alongside your own. The child does not need an account and does not get one.

You stay the account holder throughout: you decide what is recorded, what name goes on it, and who — if anyone — you share it with. You can export or delete all of it at any time. What you choose to record about your own child is your call to make.

If a child of 13 or older wants an account of their own, that is between you and them. Bear in mind that the sharing described above applies to their account the same as anyone else's.

Where your data is handled

We are based in the United States and our service providers process data in the United States. If you use the app from elsewhere, your information is transferred to and handled in the US.

Changes to this policy

If we change what we collect or who we share it with, we will update this page and change the "last updated" date above. If a change is significant, we will tell you in the app.
